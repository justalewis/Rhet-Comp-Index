Sizes and states

One accent per mark, and it is always the element carrying the meaning. Dense lists take .ic-quiet, which drops to a single colour so a sidebar of fifty-four journals does not spend the terracotta budget. .ic-absent is faience, which marks absence here as it does everywhere else in the system.

The tools

Twenty analytical tools in the five question-framed sections of /tools. Co-Citation and Bibliographic Coupling are drawn as exact inversions of one another, because that is their relationship: one converges upward on the works a third cites together, the other converges downward on the source two works share.
