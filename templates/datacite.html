Research objects on DataCite

Pinakes indexes what Crossref knows about. A great deal of the field's other work is registered with DataCite instead: datasets and corpora, software, conference talks, some theses, and articles from journals that never joined Crossref. This page counts the DataCite records that connect to the index.

Read these numbers as a lower bound. A record is here only if it cites, is cited by, or declares a relation to something already in Pinakes, was deposited by an indexed author under an ORCID, or came through a hand-kept seed list or a topical search. Most scholarly data and software in the humanities has no DOI at all, and none of that is counted.

Every record carries the reason it is here. Linked (A) records have a citation or relation tying them to the index. Corroborated (B) records came from an indexed author or a seed and also match the field's topic vocabulary. Curated records were candidates a person approved. Candidates without that support wait in a review queue and are not shown anywhere on the site. {% if overview.queue %}({{ overview.queue }} awaiting review.){% endif %}
