Feeds

One Atom feed per journal, plus one for the whole index.

Every journal Pinakes tracks has its own Atom feed of new arrivals, and there is one feed covering the whole index. Subscribe in any reader — Feedly, Inoreader, NetNewsWire, Thunderbird — and new scholarship shows up there as Pinakes indexes it.

Each feed carries the {{ feed_limit }} most recent arrivals in the order Pinakes found them, not in publication order. Much of what this index surfaces is backfilled or scraped late, so an article published in 2019 that turns up in this week's fetch is new to you even though its publication date is not.
