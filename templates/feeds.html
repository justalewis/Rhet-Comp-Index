Feeds

One feed per journal, one per section, or one built from the journals you choose.

A feed reader watches an address and tells you when something new shows up, so you don't have to keep checking the site. Pinakes publishes one address for each journal it indexes, one for each section of the field, and one covering everything.

Feeds list articles in the order Pinakes found them. A good deal of what lands here was published years earlier: a 2006 article from Open Words turned up in this June's fetch. Ordering by publication date would drop items like that far down the list, below things you read weeks ago. Each feed holds the {{ feed_limit }} most recent arrivals.

Using one of these

  1. Copy a feed address.
  2. In your feed reader, find Add, Subscribe, or +.
  3. Paste the address.

No reader yet? Feedly and Inoreader run in a browser and are free to start. NetNewsWire is a free Mac and iPhone app. Thunderbird handles feeds alongside email.

An OPML file carries the journals as separate subscriptions. Import one and each journal arrives on its own in your reader, with its own unread count. Most readers take it under Import.

Journals and sections

Every section has a ready-made feed covering all its journals, and every journal name leads to a page with that journal's own address. To cover a set that doesn't match a section, tick the journals you want: Pinakes builds one address carrying new articles from all of them. The address depends only on which journals you pick, so the same selection always produces the same feed.
