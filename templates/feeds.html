Feeds

One feed per journal, plus one for the whole index.

A feed reader watches these addresses and tells you when something new shows up, so you don't have to keep checking the site. Pinakes publishes one for each journal it indexes and one that covers everything.

Feeds list articles in the order Pinakes found them. A good deal of what lands here was published years earlier: a 2006 article from Open Words turned up in this June's fetch. Ordering by publication date would drop items like that far down the list, below things you read weeks ago. Each feed holds the {{ feed_limit }} most recent arrivals.

Using one of these

  1. Copy a feed address below.
  2. In your feed reader, find Add, Subscribe, or +.
  3. Paste the address.

No reader yet? Feedly and Inoreader run in a browser and are free to start. NetNewsWire is a free Mac and iPhone app. Thunderbird handles feeds alongside email.
