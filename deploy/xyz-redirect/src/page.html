Why the WAC Clearinghouse

Until September 2026, Pinakes ran on a single rented server that I maintained on my own via a fly.io deployment. I’m so thrilled about this recent move to the WACC for two reasons: first, the Clearinghouse gives the index a stable institutional home, something that I really couldn’t do forever. Second, it puts Pinakes beside the journals and books that Rhet/Comp scholars already read at the Clearinghouse, and I hope more of them will find the index there and use its tools to explore our web of scholarship in Writing Studies.

At CCCC in 1997, Mike Palmquist proposed a website that would provide access to scholarship on writing across the curriculum. The Clearinghouse grew out of that proposal and has published open access ever since. In its mission statement, the Clearinghouse commits itself to “barrier and cost-free access to scholarly work.” More than 200 scholars serve in its editorial roles (WOW!), and since 2024 it has been a registered nonprofit. The OA commitments of the Clearinghouse align with Pinakes’ own goal of making scholarship more discoverable and accessible, so it makes sense that it should be housed there. I’m so grateful to Mike Palmquist for making a place for Pinakes at the Clearinghouse.
